FAQ

WHAT YOU'RE
ACTUALLY SEEING.

Are the prices real?

When the data feed responds, yes. If it does not, the page shows WAITING FOR LIVE DATA instead of inventing a price.

What do the arrows do?

They only change the information inside the current monitor: Market, Signals, Thesis and Record. Scrolling changes the agent.

What is useful on each screen?

Market shows price, daily move, volume and chart. Signals shows SMA20, RSI14 and annualized short-term volatility. Thesis explains those measurements. Record will become the public history of timestamped calls after the backend is connected.

Does it trade money?

No. This stage observes and analyzes market data only. No wallets or user funds are touched.

What comes next?

Once the visual flow is approved, this repo stays connected to Vercel, while a separate Railway backend handles live data, scheduled analysis, history and the agent engine.
