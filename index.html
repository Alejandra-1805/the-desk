How do I launch?

Choose a Dot, enter a name, ticker and description, then connect your wallet. Upload your logo or use a Dot, continue to see the automatically calculated cost, then approve in your wallet. Launches use ETH on Robinhood Chain.

Where does my token appear?

After confirmation, your token CA appears in the launch review and the public catalog. Token cards open GMGN; external indexing may take time. Pons creates the trading curve and graduates completed launches to Uniswap v4.

Who can use DOT LAB?

Anyone with a compatible EVM wallet can connect. Launching requires enough ETH on Robinhood Chain and a successful permission check and simulation. Holding DOT LAB is not required.
