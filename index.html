How do I create a dot?

Choose a name and job, move the personality slider from Safe to Degen, set a budget in SOL and press Spawn Dot. Your character appears on your desk with its own profile.

Is the dot working autonomously now?

This is the interactive design version. Spawning creates the character and profile; it does not start a bot or create a wallet. Brain, activity and financial panels are ready for the live integration.

Where are tokens launched?

The intended token launch flow uses Pump.fun. DOT LAB focuses on agents, profiles, data and activity. Token creation and rewards are not enabled in this version.

Is DOT LAB an OpenAI product?

No. This is an independent project, not affiliated with OpenAI.
