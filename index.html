How does the launch work?

Choose a Dot, add a token name, ticker, description and artwork, then review your project. Copy the details and download the artwork. Continue to the official Pump.fun creation page to upload them, connect your Solana wallet and sign.

What does DOT LAB charge?

DOT LAB currently adds no launch fee. Pump.fun's published coin-creation fee is 0 SOL; Solana network costs and any optional initial purchase are separate. Review the final costs before signing on Pump.fun.

Which wallet is used?

Your own Solana wallet signs on Pump.fun. There are no agent wallets and DOT LAB does not hold your funds or private keys.

Can I launch directly inside this page?

This version prepares your launch and opens Pump.fun to complete it. An integrated launch with signing inside DOT LAB is not enabled yet. Token details are not automatically transferred to Pump.fun.

What does choosing a Dot do?

It selects your project's visual theme and its PC artwork. You can upload a separate square image as the token's logo.
